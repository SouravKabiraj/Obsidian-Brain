As Tech Lead for our Bullet Loan product, I worked with Product, Design, Payments, and Deposits teams to build a new short-term lending solution. Early challenges included siloed planning—Payments had API delays, Deposits needed new disbursement logic, and Design’s UX assumptions didn’t account for backend constraints. 

**To fix this, I led a cross-functional roadmap session to align timelines, established shared KPIs (like disbursement success rate), and introduced biweekly demos for faster feedback.** 

As a result, we launched 3 weeks early, achieved 95% disbursement reliability, and grew short-term loan volume by 20%. The process also strengthened trust between teams for future projects.
