**Project Nugget:** Broke silos and aligned cross-functional teams to launch Bullet Loan product.

### **S – Situation**

As Tech Lead for the Bullet Loan product, I collaborated with Product, Design, Payments, and Deposits teams. Early in the project, we ran into alignment issues—Payments had API delays, Deposits needed new disbursement logic, and Design’s UX didn’t reflect backend limitations.

### **T – Task**

I needed to unify these teams under a shared plan to avoid delays, ensure technical feasibility, and deliver a reliable short-term lending product.

### **A – Action**

- Led a **cross-functional roadmap alignment session** to sync timelines and clarify dependencies
- Established **shared KPIs** like disbursement success rate to keep everyone outcome-focused
- Introduced **biweekly demo reviews** for faster feedback and real-time problem solving
- Acted as a bridge between teams to resolve technical misunderstandings quickly
### **R – Result**

- We launched the product **3 weeks early**
- Hit **95% disbursement reliability** at launch
- Increased short-term loan volume by **20%**
- The initiative also built lasting **trust and coordination habits** across teams
