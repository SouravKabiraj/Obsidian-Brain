At a high level product features are to be prioritized by aligning closely to the program's KPIs (improving CSAT or revenue, etc.), while checking the technical feasibility and efforts required.

Since we are taking about competing priorities the better way to take a decision will be a data driven approach. In this scenario, we can use the weighted scoring model where a numerical value is assigned to each of the critical decision taking factor so that a consensus can be arrived. This model gives us the flexibility to define the decision making factors that are to be considered while resolving the conflict, such as as customer impact, technical feasibility and revenue generating opportunity, etc.
