
**Situation** was that there was a release planned and release dates were planned much ahead of time. Team was working hard to deliver on time. I had brought up code quality concerns based on my observation. 

**Task** : There was a constant push from business and product team to launch 0 to 1 product as soon as possible. I need to show the downside of writing non scalable code.

**Action** : 
I took help from performance testing team to do a performance testing with 1 million, gathered critical failure information from the testing report.
I had gathered data from the quality engineering and development team ahead of time to line up different tasks to address quality concerns and gathered estimates around it. 
I listed few upcoming non-critical feature which can be deprioritize.
I reported this data up to the leadership after meeting with team and requested for more time (new projected date) due to product quality concerns.

**Result** : Leadership agreed to delay and we eventually addressed quality concerns prior to the release.
