**Situation:**  
At Grab, we were integrating a new payment provider into the GrabPay Wallet using the low-code integration framework I had built. The original timeline estimated two weeks for completion.

**Task:**  
I was responsible for ensuring a seamless integration, optimising API interactions, and handling compliance requirements. However, midway through the project, we encountered unexpected regulatory changes that required additional security checks and modifications.

**Action:**  
Instead of rushing to meet the deadline with incomplete compliance, I proactively worked with the legal and security teams to implement the necessary changes. I also communicated the risks and timeline adjustments to stakeholders, ensuring transparency. To recover lost time, I streamlined testing with automation, enabling faster iterations once compliance was met.

**Result:**  
Although the project was delayed by one week, the additional compliance measures ensured a smooth launch without regulatory roadblocks. In the long run, these enhancements improved the framework’s flexibility for future integrations. This experience reinforced the importance of risk assessment, proactive communication, and balancing speed with quality.
