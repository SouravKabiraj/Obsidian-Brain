As a Tech Lead, I once received feedback that I was too prescriptive in code reviews, which stifled collaboration. Initially, I felt defensive, but I took time to reflect and discussed it further with the engineer. I realised that while I was focused on maintaining standards, I wasn’t fostering enough teamwork. I encourage the team to learn coding principal and setup a bi-weekly meeting to discuss one coding principal.
I adjusted my approach by framing feedback as suggestions and encouraging more pair programming. This led to stronger team engagement and better technical outcomes.
