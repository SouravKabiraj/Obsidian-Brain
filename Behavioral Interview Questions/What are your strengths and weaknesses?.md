This is a great question! If you don't mind, I'd love to list 3 strengths and weaknesses then dive a little deeper on one of each of them.

I think my three greatest strengths are:

- Empathy
- Openness to feedback
- Drive

And three weaknesses I have been working to strengthen are:

- Sometimes my desire to achieve can put me at risk of overcommitting
- My engineering mind starts solving problems during problem discussion, due to that I used to miss minor details.
- Creating detailed and strategic lists of priorities so I can meet the needs of my team, cross-functional teammates and senior leadership.

**Empathy** is probably my greatest strength as an Engineering Lead and teammate. I often lead with empathy and curiosity whether I'm having a 1:1 with at team member and chatting about a tough project they're working through, or having a performance management conversation. Over time, showing that I care not only about their experience as a professional at our company but their happiness and fulfillment as a whole person builds trust and creates the foundation for those more difficult conversations to build relationship and lead to positive change. I like to explicitly state my values and the values for my team (empathy and is one of these) to the staff I supervise and in a team setting to create clarity and alignment about who I am as a person and leader and the type of culture I'd like us to build on our team.
