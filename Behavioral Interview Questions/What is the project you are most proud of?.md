**Situation:**  
At Grab, integrating a new payment method was a time-consuming process, taking over two months per integration. This created bottlenecks for expanding GrabPay's ecosystem and slowed down market expansion.

**Task:**  
I was tasked with designing a scalable solution that would streamline the integration process, enabling faster onboarding of multiple payment partners while ensuring reliability and security.

**Action:**  
I designed and built a **low-code wallet integration framework**, making it easy for developers to onboard new payment providers with minimal effort. This enterprise-grade software standardised integration workflows, reduced manual effort, and automated key processes.

**Result:**
- Reduced integration time **from 2 months to just 1 week**
- Successfully onboarded **15+ payment partners** (e.g., Zalopay, GCash)
- Scaled to **10M+ daily transactions**
- Grab **applied for a patent** for this framework
- I received a **promotion** in recognition of this achievement
