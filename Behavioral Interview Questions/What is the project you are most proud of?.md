**Project Nugget:** Built a low-code wallet integration framework that transformed payment partner onboarding.

### **S – Situation**

At Grab, integrating a new payment method took over **2 months**, causing delays in expanding the GrabPay ecosystem and limiting our speed to launch in new markets.

### **T – Task**

I was tasked with creating a **scalable, secure, and developer-friendly solution** that could drastically reduce the integration time for external wallet and payment providers.

### **A – Action**

- Designed and implemented a **low-code integration framework** that standardized workflows, automated validation, and minimized manual engineering work
- Focused on security, modularity, and extensibility to future-proof the system
- Onboarded early feedback from partner engineers to improve dev experience
- Created detailed SDKs and dashboards for observability and fast iteration

### **R – Result**

- Cut integration time from **2 months to 1 week**
- Enabled onboarding of **15+ payment partners** (e.g., GCash, Zalopay)
- Scaled to handle over **10M daily transactions**
- Grab **filed a patent** for the framework
- I was **promoted** based on the impact of this project
