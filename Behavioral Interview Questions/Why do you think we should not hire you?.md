**Project Nugget:** Self-awareness of strengths—root cause thinker over volume coder.

### **S – Situation**

In many fast-paced environments, there’s a tendency to equate impact with how much code one produces. I've seen teams reward speed over systems thinking, often leading to tech debt or misaligned outcomes.

### **T – Task**

I’ve had to reflect on what kind of environment helps me thrive—and where I might not be the best fit.

### **A – Action**

I’ve learned that I bring the most value in roles where:

- I can focus on **solving the root problem**, not just treating symptoms
    
- There’s space for **designing scalable systems** and **aligning with stakeholders**
    
- I can help bridge engineering execution with product strategy
    

That said, if the main expectation is to push code fast without context or collaboration, then I may not align with those priorities.

### **R – Result**

In roles that value **thoughtful architecture, strategic collaboration, and long-term impact**, I’ve consistently delivered scalable solutions, built strong cross-functional trust, and earned recognition—including promotions and patent-worthy contributions.
