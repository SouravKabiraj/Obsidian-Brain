# How to structure the answer
Use STAR method to structure the answer 

# How to prepare
Try to prepare a story which involves each one of the principles.
### Customer Obsession
1. Tell me about a time where you faced a very difficult customer.
2. Tell me about a time where you have negotiated a win-win situation.
### Ownership
1) Tell me a tough decision you made in project.
2) Describe a tough situation in which you had to step into a leadership role.
### Invent and Simplify
1) Tell me about a time where you solved a complex problem
2) How you handle road blocks 

### Are Right, A Lot
1) Tell me about a time when you had to make a decision without much customer data
2) Tell me about a time when you have to convince teammates on somehing you proposed
### Learn and Be Curious
1) Tell me about a time when you build a process for your team
2) Tell me about a skill you recently learned

### Hire and Develop the Best
1) Tell me about a time when you have conflict with someone and how you resolved it and what did you learned

### Insist on the Highest Standards
1) Tell me about a time when you had to make short term sacrifice to meet long term goal.
2) Tell me about a time when you made a decision on the basis of data but you were wrong
### Think Big
1) Tell me about your greatest success
2) Tell me about a time when you are creative

### Bias for Action
1) How have you convinced other to take action
2) How have you managed risk in a project
### Frugality
1) Tell me about a time you turned down more resources to complete a project
2) Tell me about a time when you accomplished a big project with less resource
### Earn Trust
1) How you earn the trust of your team member
2) How you managed conflicts
### Dive Deep
1) Tell me about the most complex project you worked on
2) Have you changed an opinion using data

### Have Backbone; Disagree and Commit
1) How do you manage difficult conversion 
2) Tell me about a time when you have disagreement with your manager
### Deliver Results
1) Tell me about a time when you worked on a challenging project and how you worked on
2) How do you prioritize projects
### Strive to be Earth’s Best Employer
1) Tell me about a time you motivate a team after demoralizing event 
2) Tell me about a time an employee give me negative feedback
### Success and Scale Bring Broad Responsibility
1) Tell me about a time when you have a problem and how you discover a real cause
2) Describe a time when your project failed


# Conclusion
- Don't repeat stories
- Share tread-offs
- Be a good storyteller
